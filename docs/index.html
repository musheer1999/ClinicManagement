Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance and can be cancelled anytime — cancellation stops the next billing cycle, with continued access until the end of the current paid period.
  • If a payment is charged in error or a duplicate charge occurs, contact us within 7 days of the transaction for a full refund.
  • Refund requests for service issues are reviewed case by case; approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • No refunds are issued for partial months of usage on voluntary cancellation.

Privacy Policy

ClinicDesk stores clinic and patient data (names, contact details, visit and prescription records) to provide the service.

  • Each clinic's data is isolated and accessible only to that clinic's authenticated users — no cross-clinic data sharing.
  • Data is used solely to operate the platform (patient records, PDF generation, OTP login) and is not sold to third parties.
  • Payment information is processed by our payment partner (Razorpay) and is not stored on ClinicDesk's servers.
  • Clinics may request export or deletion of their data by contacting us below.
